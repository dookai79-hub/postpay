Rules and Privacy Policy

Rules

  • You must be 18 years or older to use PostPay.
  • Adult, sexual, violent, hateful, illegal or stolen content is not allowed. This includes nudity and anything that sexualises minors.
  • Links in text posts are not allowed (to stop scams). Videos can only be added through the YouTube link box.
  • Photos are checked automatically before posting, and posts with banned words are blocked. Videos must be on YouTube, and age-restricted videos are not allowed.
  • Accounts that break these rules are permanently banned and unpaid earnings can be withheld.
  • Only post photos and videos that are yours or that you have permission to use. You are responsible for everything you post.
  • Tap "Report" on any post that breaks the rules. A post with 3 reports is hidden automatically. The admin can remove posts that break the rules.

Earnings

  • 30% of each month's ad revenue is shared among creators based on views on photo and video posts. Text-only posts do not earn.
  • One person counts as 1 view per post per day. Views on your own posts are not counted.
  • When your earnings reach Rs 100 or more, send a "Payment request". Payment is made by UPI. If you earn less, it carries over to next month.
  • Payments are made only after real ad revenue is received. Earnings are not guaranteed.
  • Your share will be withheld if fake views or stolen content are found.

Privacy Policy

  • Login is through Google. Our database stores your Google name, your Google profile photo and a unique ID.
  • Videos you upload are stored with our video partner, Cloudinary.
  • Your UPI ID is saved only if you enter it, and is visible only to the admin for payments.
  • Your posts (name, text, photo, video) are visible to everyone.
  • Ad networks on this site (such as Adsterra) may use cookies or device information.
  • We do not sell your data.
